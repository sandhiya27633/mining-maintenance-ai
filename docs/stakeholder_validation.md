## Purpose
The purpose of stakeholder validation is to align the prototype functionality, dashboard presentation, and the AI duty-cycle recommendations with the domain expertise of actual mining industry professionals.

*(Note: The DCSS weights used in this prototype are configuration values for demonstration and have not been validated against real mining data.)*

## Intended Stakeholder Roles
- **Dispatcher:** Manages immediate daily vehicle assignments and handles real-time disruptions.
- **Fleet Manager:** Oversees overall fleet availability, KPIs, and overarching vehicle health.
- **Maintenance Planner:** Schedules and coordinates the execution of maintenance based on recommended intervals.
- **Domain Expert:** Validates the Duty-Cycle Severity Score (DCSS) weight configuration against physical reality.

## Validation Questions
1. How useful is the dashboard in surfacing immediate, actionable insights for your daily workflow?
2. Is the explanation of the Duty-Cycle Severity Score (DCSS) clear and understandable?
3. How useful are the maintenance interval recommendations provided by the DCSS model?
4. Do the recommended intervals (e.g., extensions for low severity, reductions for high severity) feel appropriate?
5. Does the override workflow meet your requirements for adjusting maintenance schedules?
6. What additional information, if any, is required when submitting a dispatcher override?
7. Is the override audit history tracking sufficient for accountability?
8. Are the risk-level alerts (e.g., CRITICAL vs HIGH) distinct and appropriately severe?
9. What additional data points do you need for optimal decision-making that are currently missing?
10. What are your top suggestions for improving the overall usability of the maintenance planner?
